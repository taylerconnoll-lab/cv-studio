Imported PDF

Your PDF looks exactly as it did. Click any line of text to edit it in place; the original only changes where you edit. Drag the grip above a line to move that line, and use the toolbar for font, size and colour.

Sections and entries

Move whole sections, or whole entries (role, company, date and bullets move together), with the arrows below or the buttons above a line on the page. Use ＋ to add an entry in the same format as the others. If a heading or entry was missed, hover its line and press H or E.

Highlights and colours

Click a highlight, line or colour block on the page, or use this list, to change its colour, move it, resize it or delete it. Handy for removing a coloured band or a highlight you don't want.

Spacing
Click any text to edit. Select text to change its font, size or weight.
